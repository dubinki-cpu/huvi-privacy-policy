Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Huvi and Sea Beasts mobile application (the "Application"), developed by Shimix Games (the "Service Provider") and made available as a free service. The Application is provided "AS IS".

1. Information We Collect

To provide gameplay, online features, and account services, the Application collects the following information:

Account Information

  • Email address (used for account registration and login via Firebase Authentication)
  • Password (encrypted and managed by Firebase Authentication; never visible to the Service Provider)
  • User ID (a unique identifier assigned by Firebase)

Profile and Gameplay Data

  • Player name (chosen by the user, displayed in chat, leaderboards, and brotherhoods)
  • Selected avatar
  • Game progress: coins, level, experience points, depth, inventory, equipment upgrades, selected sea
  • VS mode score and prize history
  • Daily Quest, Daily Spin and reward progress
  • Brotherhood (guild) membership and role
  • In-app purchase records (product identifiers and purchase state, processed by Google Play Billing)

User-Generated Content

  • Chat messages sent in World Chat and Team Chat
  • Free-text messages you send to in-game fisherman characters (see "AI-Generated Content" below)
  • Brotherhood names and descriptions (if you create a brotherhood)
  • Reports you submit about other users (for moderation purposes)
  • List of users you have blocked

Advertising Data

  • The Application shows optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob). To serve these ads, Google may collect the device advertising ID and related ad-serving data in accordance with the Google Ads privacy policy. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your precise or approximate device location
  • We do not collect your phone number or contacts
  • We do not collect photos, videos, audio, or files from your device
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties

2. AI-Generated Content

The Application includes an optional feature that lets you talk to in-game fisherman characters. Free-text messages you send to these characters, together with the last few messages of that conversation, are processed by Google Gemini (a generative AI service provided by Google) in order to generate the character's reply. These messages are:

  • Sent from our game server to Google's API for the sole purpose of generating a reply
  • Not linked to your name, email address, or account by us; they are transmitted without personal identifiers
  • Not stored by the Service Provider after the reply is generated
  • Filtered for inappropriate language before being sent, and generated under content-safety rules suitable for a general audience

Character replies are automatically generated and may occasionally be inaccurate. Please do not share personal information in these conversations. Use of this feature is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and applicable Gemini API terms. This feature is limited to a small number of messages per day per player.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Account management — creating, authenticating, and maintaining your account
  • App functionality — saving game progress to the cloud so it persists across devices
  • Multiplayer features — enabling VS matches, brotherhoods, leaderboards, and chat
  • AI character chat — generating replies from in-game characters, as described in Section 2
  • Rewarded advertising — showing optional ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards
  • In-app purchases — delivering items you purchase and restoring purchases
  • Moderation — processing user reports and blocked-user lists to keep the community safe

We do not use your data for marketing or third-party analytics.

4. Data Storage and Security

Account and progress data is stored on Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database). Real-time multiplayer matches and the AI character chat are handled by a game server operated by the Service Provider (running the open-source Nakama server software, hosted in the European Union). Data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption. The Service Provider applies physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards consistent with industry practice to protect your information.

5. Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own Privacy Policy:

  • Google Play Services (including Google Play Billing for in-app purchases)
  • Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database)
  • Google AdMob (rewarded video ads)
  • Google Gemini API (AI character replies, see Section 2)

The Service Provider may disclose information:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process
  • When in good faith we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud
  • With trusted service providers (such as Firebase) acting on our behalf and bound by confidentiality obligations

6. Your Data Rights and Account Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time.

How to Request Account and Data Deletion

To delete your Huvi and Sea Beasts account and all associated data, you have two options:

  1. Visit our dedicated account deletion page:
    https://dubinki-cpu.github.io/huvi-privacy-policy/delete-account.html
  2. Send an email to dubinki@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account". Please include the email address used to register and your in-game player name (if known).

We will process your deletion request and remove your account within 30 days.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Account email and authentication credentials
  • Player name, avatar, and profile data
  • All game progress: coins, level, inventory, upgrades
  • VS mode score history and unclaimed prizes
  • Brotherhood membership records
  • Chat messages you sent in World Chat and Team Chat
  • Daily Quest and Daily Spin progress
  • Your blocked users list
  • Your game-server profile used for multiplayer and AI chat

What May Be Retained

  • Reports you submitted about other users (kept anonymized for moderation review)
  • Records required by applicable law, tax obligations, or fraud prevention

Other Rights

In addition to deletion, you may request:

  • Access — a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — fixing any inaccurate data

To exercise these rights, contact dubinki@gmail.com.

7. Opt-Out

You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Application using the standard uninstall process on your mobile device. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete your cloud-stored account; to remove your account data, follow the deletion process in Section 6. You can also avoid the AI character chat and rewarded ads entirely by simply not using those optional features.

8. Data Retention

The Service Provider retains user-provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. If you wish to have your data removed sooner, please follow the deletion procedure in Section 6.

9. Children

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, and is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact dubinki@gmail.com and we will promptly delete the information from our servers.

10. Security

The Service Provider is committed to safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. We use industry-standard physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect data we process and maintain. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
