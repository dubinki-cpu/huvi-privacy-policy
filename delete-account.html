Account Deletion Request

App: Huvi and Sea Beasts
Developer: Shimix Games

How to Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your Huvi and Sea Beasts account and all associated data, please send an email to:

dubinki@gmail.com

Subject line: Delete My Account

Please include in the email body:

  • The email address used to register in the game
  • Your in-game player name (if you remember it)

We will process your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Account email and authentication credentials
  • Player name, avatar selection, and profile data
  • Game progress: coins, level, inventory, upgrades
  • VS mode score and prizes
  • Brotherhood (guild) membership data
  • Chat messages sent by you (World Chat and Team Chat)
  • Daily Quest progress
  • Blocked users list and any reports submitted

What Data May Be Retained

  • Reports you submitted about other users (kept for moderation, anonymized)
  • Aggregated/anonymized analytics that cannot be tied back to you
  • Records required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes

For more information, please see our Privacy Policy.
